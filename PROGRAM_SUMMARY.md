# Josh Program Workout Summary

This is the workout-only distillation of the pasted syllabus. Readings, videos, listening, and browse assignments were removed.

## Structure

- Length: 9 weeks
- Frequency: 4 days per week
- Training days: Monday, Tuesday, Thursday, Friday
- Split: Upper / Lower
- Total sessions: 36
- Session rotation: Upper 1, Lower 1, Upper 2, Lower 2, Upper 3, Lower 3, then repeat through week 9 as prescribed

## Training Notes

- Most movements are machine or isolation-focused.
- For each new movement, use the first week to find a comfortable load for the assigned rep range.
- Record weights and reps every week.
- When reps increase, keep last week's weight and try to add reps.
- When reps decrease, increase the weight for that set.
- Stop a set at technical failure or when form changes enough to require a major posture shift.
- Use controlled tempo and full range of motion.
- Warm up before work sets by reducing the first work-set weight by about 50%.
- Back-off sets appear later in the program. For those, reduce the third-set weight by about 10-20%.

## App Encoding

The exact 36-session sequence, exercises, rep targets, and back-off markers are encoded in `index.html` as the `PROGRAM` array.
